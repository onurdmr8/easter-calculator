# Privacy Policy for Easter Calculator

**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

Easter Calculator ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Easter Calculator mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Authentication Data
- **Anonymous Guest Mode:** When you use the App as a guest, a random unique identifier (UUID) is generated locally and stored via Firebase Anonymous Authentication to track your discoveries and leaderboard rank. No personal information is collected.
- **Google Sign-In & Email/Password:** If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google or Email, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication user ID (UID) provided by Google Firebase Authentication.

### B. Game Progress & Leaderboard Data
- Discovered Easter Eggs, timestamps, total scores, and monthly season rankings are stored in Google Cloud Firestore to maintain the global leaderboard and sync your progress across devices.

### C. Device & Diagnostics Data
- Basic technical information (such as operating system version, app crashes) may be collected automatically by Google Firebase to ensure app stability and performance.

---

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected information solely to:
- Provide and maintain the calculator and game features.
- Calculate and display global leaderboard standings.
- Allow seamless account linking and score persistence.
- Prevent fraudulent or automated score submissions.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App integrates services provided by Google LLC:
- **Google Play Services**
- **Firebase Authentication**
- **Cloud Firestore**
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**

These services operate under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated leaderboard data at any time by contacting us or resetting your profile within the app.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Easter Calculator is a general-audience educational and puzzle calculator application. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent.
